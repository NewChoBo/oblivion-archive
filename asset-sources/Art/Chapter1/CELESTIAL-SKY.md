# 성을 둘러싼 밤하늘

내장 ImageGen으로 챕터 1의 심우주 배경을 그렸다. 현재 게임은 1024×1024 PNG 여섯 장을 큐브맵으로 읽는다. 4096×2048 파노라마도 함께 만들어 다른 엔진에서 같은 방향으로 배치할 수 있게 했다. [스카이박스 파일과 방향표](skybox/README.md)

전망탑 위의 은하 띠는 배경 전체에서 좁은 영역을 차지한다. 떨어져 있는 작은 별이 천천히 반짝이도록 하늘과 우주 생명체가 같은 큐브맵 샘플을 사용한다. 이 연출의 밝기 변화는 배경에만 적용한다.

## 큐브맵 원화 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Produce a very high detail, 2:1 horizontal astronomical matte painting that will be projected on the west part of a high-resolution cubemap skybox in a dark-fantasy game. A magnificent but remote, fragile diagonal filament of an ancient nebula hangs in endless black space. Deep ink-black surrounds the detailed central band, with a handful of tiny white and ice-blue stars suspended well away from the gas. Show fine turbulent filaments, sharp individual stellar pinpoints, layered wisps of slate blue, restrained amethyst and faint silver, delicate transitions, granular dust lanes with a sense of extreme physical scale. Only the very center and a few real stellar cores have luminous accents. The cloudy material stays airy, narrow and shadowed. Composition: brightest feature around 49% horizontally and 34% from the top, with the filament drifting from lower left to upper right. Keep empty black below it, isolated small star points above it, and fade the outer 15% on every edge softly to uniform near-black for technical blending into the rest of the skybox. This is an ultra-detailed sky texture, fine-grained and photographic in the manner of astronomical deep-field imagery, not a poster, watercolour, swirl logo, visible planet, black-hole ring or horizon. No castle, foreground, labels, text, border or illustration frame. All the surrounding darkness should feel genuinely immense, quiet and ominous.

## 초기 어두운 파노라마 편집 프롬프트

[어두운 파노라마](abyssal-sky.webp) · [초기 파노라마](celestial-sky.webp)

Use case: lighting-weather. Refine the supplied 2:1 equirectangular, seamless, 360-degree deep-space sky texture for an isolated dark-fantasy castle. Preserve the exact 2:1 projection, the placement of the existing galactic arc near the central longitude, and the principle of a sky-only texture wrapping all around. Reimagine the lighting and density to evoke the fear of looking down into an immeasurably ancient, endless cosmic abyss. Make about three quarters of the panorama practically empty ink-black with a faint cool indigo cast and only very widely spaced, tiny stars. The entire lower third should fall away into black open depth. In the central longitude, from roughly 37–61% across and 30–51% down, retain only a slender, ragged and discontinuous veil of distant nebula, its largest dark dust lane dissolving into the abyss. A few fine, opalescent filaments and restrained pinpoints of frost-blue, muted silver-lilac and an occasional dim amber star create moments of delicate beauty. One or two especially beautiful small stars are enough. The silver nebula highlights stay mostly midtone; only the tiny star cores approach white. A feeling of looking very far into the unknown, still, silent, vast, and unsettling. Let empty black space surround the arc vertically and horizontally. Very dark, nearly matching left and right edges for an invisible wrap, and extremely dark flat poles. No sun, no planet, no horizon line, no black-hole circle or glowing ring, no monsters, no clouds like Earth's sky, no storm, no bright watercolor wash, no text, no buildings, no foreground, no borders. Retain natural fine nebula detail and graceful gradation within the narrow luminous region. Return only the full sky texture.

## 초기 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a 2:1 equirectangular seamless 360-degree sky texture for a dark-fantasy game about a solitary medieval castle suspended in deep space. Show the sky texture only, edge to edge, with no land, castle, foreground, planets, or text. Palette: deep midnight navy and indigo, glints of ice-blue and violet, a restrained pearly silver core and tiny amber stars. The primary feature is a long irregular arc of luminous galactic dust and fine clouds, drifting across the central third of the sky and rising toward the right, like a subtle Milky Way with distinct dark dust lanes and blue-lilac wisps. Keep the brightest structure near image center horizontally at roughly 44 percent down from the top. Add a subdued second veil of very faint blue clouds to its side, and varied sparse fine pinprick stars throughout. Use a few jewel-like bright stars with delicate soft optical glows, nothing oversized. Art direction is mysterious and quiet, detailed painterly nebula structure with natural deep space color gradations, strong depth and contrast, dark enough that torch-lit architecture in front will read. Keep the leftmost and rightmost 8 percent both very dark with matching sparse stars and no large bright features to minimize a longitude seam. Fade both top and bottom poles gently to a uniform deep navy to avoid sampling distortion. No horizon, no vignetting frame, no logos, no borders or infographic. The composition should still feel expansive and striking through a roughly 80-degree camera field of view.

## 전망탑에서 보이는 존재

초기 테스트는 내장 ImageGen으로 만든 [실루엣 WebP](void-silhouette.webp)로 성운과 별빛을 가렸다. 현재 장면의 3D 모델과 콘셉트는 [괴물 제작 기록](../../../docs/art/chapter-1/README.md#괴물)에 있다.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a solitary hand-shaped silhouette of an immense cosmic organism used as an alpha occlusion matte in a dark-fantasy 3D game sky. Transparent background. The composition is a horizontal profile, broad and graceful at the top, with an asymmetric flattened central mass like a swimming aquatic leviathan viewed at an extreme distance, gradually tapering to an indistinct rear. A few narrow, very long, irregular dangling filament-like appendages should trail below and behind, subtle and legible at distance. Make this feel ancient, alien and unknowably massive, only intermittently recognizable as alive, like a deep-sea sighting rather than a character render. A nearly black opaque silhouette with organic, slightly ragged contours, no illuminated surface detail, no eyes, no lights, no outline, no spikes of pure fantasy ornament, no gore, no surroundings, no atoms or nebula, no text. Keep the whole shape fully inside the image with a 10% transparent margin on every side, fill most of the landscape width. One isolated shape with natural translucent tips fading into genuine transparent alpha. This is intended to gently eclipse a distant nebula and stars as it crosses the view.
