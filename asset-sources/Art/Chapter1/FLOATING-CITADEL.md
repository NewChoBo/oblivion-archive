현재 원화는 작업 중인 성의 용도를 드러낸다. 하역 창고에서 책 상자를 내리고 제본 공방에서 수선한 뒤 대서고에 보관한다. 대서고 서쪽은 열람동이고 동쪽은 원본 서고다. 천문대는 서쪽 끝 한 곳에 둔다. 원화는 이름 복원이 끝난 성을 그린다.

## 현재 원화 생성 프롬프트

A fresh professional game environment keyframe/concept art for a richly inhabited monumental floating archive citadel in a dark starry fantasy void. Cinematic 16:9 full-bleed, elevated bird's eye three-quarter view across the main approach. Architectural focus and genuinely different building purposes, a cluster of usable buildings not terraces. The path leads from a tapered arrival islet in the lower left through a tall three arch curving stone bridge and twin cylindrical gate towers into a fan-shaped civic court. The dominant landmark is a HUGE, deep and WIDE cathedral-sized LIBRARY complex at the highest northern end, central great nave with an amber glowing Gothic stellar window flanked by two long pitched-roof STACK WINGS, layered clerestories, buttresses, reading hall and cross gables. It should feel about 4 times the size/volume of any ordinary building, long side elevation and multiple projecting wings clearly seen, not a standalone chapel. Along the left/west side of the square is a BROAD two-bay bindery workshop with lower warm brick and slate sawtooth roofs, an open door, hanging folios, workbench and drying frames, a chimney; behind it off to the westernmost edge a single small open round astronomical tower is the only observatory. Along the right/east side of the square sits a practical FREIGHT DEPOT connected to the court, a deep pitched slate-roof warehouse with a broad open bay, shipping crates, visible projecting timber cargo crane with hanging chain over the void and a tethered very small cargo gondola; it is clearly for unloading archive books. These buildings are on sturdy stone foundations with naturally curving curtain walls, on different shelf heights. Clear architectural hierarchy, readable silhouettes, traversable paths, sweeping two-flight staircase elevating to library, fractured rock foundations beneath. Moongrey limestone, deep desaturated blue roofs, amber windows, muted red pennants, cinematic graphic-novel painterly hard-edged values with luminous edge accents. High quality original game production concept, strong silhouettes legible at thumbnail, fine but controlled details. No lettering, no labels, no collage, no split panels, no UI, no explanatory lines. Show all buildings entirely within frame, crisp enough to serve as a modeling guide.

## 첫 배치 원화 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Production environment key art for Oblivion Archive, a dark fantasy exploration game. Design a completely new monumental floating archive citadel as a level designer would, with a strong identifiable silhouette and coherent places to walk. Wide landscape oblique bird's-eye establishing shot looking inland from the arrival island; show the entire continuous route and both ends of the island in one image. Foreground: a small teardrop arrival headland, then a long, gently bending stone viaduct on three colossal open arches over a star-filled gulf. Midground: a gatehouse with twin ROUND cylindrical drum towers carrying garnet pennants opens into a broad fan-shaped court, low crenellations flowing around the irregular cliff edge. One side branches along a sinuous wall-walk to a round open stargazing bastion; opposite is a high arcaded gallery. Far end: stairs to a towering gothic scriptorium with a magnificent amber lancet window and four unequal pinnacles. Use broken curved stretches, offsets, circular towers and changing wall heights; avoid squared-off box fortresses and a uniformly symmetric grid. Jagged rock hanging beneath the castle, sparse stars and quiet teal nebula in deep black space, cool carved slate, muted brass, a few amber windows, tiny indistinct figures for scale. Readable sculptural masses and an elegant hand-painted, stylized 3D game aesthetic. Bright enough to read the stone tiers and distinguish the paths. No labels, borders, map graphics or typography.
