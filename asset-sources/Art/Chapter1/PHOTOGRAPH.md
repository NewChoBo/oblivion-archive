## 복원본 생성 프롬프트

Full bleed landscape 4:3 archival portrait, one flat in-world photographic print texture for a dark fantasy cel-shaded game. Two adult library workers side by side in a posed waist-up portrait at the last reading, the left older woman librarian with silver hair gathered up and a high collar holding a closed folio, the right younger male scribe Ian with short black hair, a dark mantle and a pale quill in his hand. Distinct heads and shoulders with a clear hand-width gap between their silhouettes. The right figure occupies only the right half. Original hand-inked illustration made to look like a hand-tinted antique silver gelatin print; clean, broad graphic shadow shapes and strong silhouettes, subtle halftone grain, subdued warm ivory, ink charcoal, smoky petrol blue and aged silver. One tall, softly lit stone arch behind them, simple defocused shelves. Quiet, slightly wistful expressions. Large readable faces, gently worn pale corners, no words, no lettering, no frame, no desk, no border outside the image, no text panels, no collage. Edge-to-edge flat scanned print, straight-on.

## 지워진 사진 편집 프롬프트

Edit this exact archival photograph into the erased version of the same in-game print. Keep identical 4:3 framing, the entire left woman librarian, her features, folds of clothes and book, the arch, background surroundings, palette, border and lighting. Change only the man on the right: he has been supernaturally rubbed out of the emulsion. His entire figure including the quill is now a softly rubbed irregular pale silver paper silhouette with a few fine charcoal scuffs and softly feathered ink edges, clearly head-and-shoulders shaped, so even at low resolution it is obvious a person is missing. No facial features or hand outlines or quill details left inside that empty space. No new objects, labels, or writing. Keep all other details as close as possible, same vantage point and subject sizes.
