# Chapter 1 expedition loop

Base: `be31dcf`. Implementation: `src/expedition/`, assembled by `main.ts`; existing chapter progression, codex schema and save keys remain intact.

## Playing

- Start Chapter 1 normally. The west chest beside the entrance contains a survey wand, 12 reserve crystals, 10 arrows and 25 armor, once per new game. Tab opens/closes the bag; an existing/custom I assignment also works.
- Worn slots, the six-slot carry bag, personal storage and story records have separate lists in one fixed-size dialog. Equip the survey wand to move the original wand into the bag. The torch stays worn. Storage deposits and withdrawals require standing beside the east entrance chest.
- Mira stands west of the entrance, at `[-4.5, -2.5]` on the floor plan. Talk with E for chapter route guidance, full health recovery and the optional three-kill contract. The chapter puzzle requires no hunting, consumable or skill purchase.
- The blue portal behind Mira, at `[-4.5, .5]`, leads to the physically separate ink quarry. Its return portal is at `[64, -8]`; the south entry is safe from enemy attacks. Three ink apparitions have actual health, pursuit, attacks, stun and kill rewards. A field chest near the north-east wall contains the hunter bow and another one-time supply reward.
- Each kill grants 30 XP, 5 reserve crystals, 4 arrows and 8 armor. Re-entering through the portal creates a new three-enemy wave. Returning to Mira after three kills pays one extra skill point and repairs armor once.
- The growth tab spends skill points on damage (+25% per rank), maximum health (+20 per rank) or armor absorption/capacity (+10 percentage points and +15 capacity per rank). Each has three ranks. XP thresholds are 60, 120, 180, etc.; each level grants one point.
- Wand ammunition starts at 6 loaded + 18 reserve; the bow starts at 1 + 16. Recharging/nocking transfers existing reserves into the weapon. Spent ammunition is never regenerated by a reload, retry or continue. R is the default reload key, and hints respect reassignment. With both ammunition types completely exhausted, Mira provides one small emergency supply; further conversations grant nothing until it has actually been spent.
- Armor initially has 40/60 durability and absorbs 50% of each hit until depleted. It is consumed by that absorbed damage; the remainder reduces health. The armor skill increases absorption to at most 80%.
- Esc pauses/resumes play, or closes the current inventory/settings interface. Menu, inventory and settings share jump pause, audio pause, input deactivation and canvas inertness. Other open dialogs (including the coordinator's prologue) own their own keys.

## Saving and integration

`oblivion-archive.expedition.v1` stores inventory locations, worn gear, selected weapon, torch light, both ammunition pools, chest/contract claims, XP, skills and the current hunting wave. Every resource change saves the whole ledger immediately. The chapter's existing `oblivion-archive.chapter1.v1` and per-entry codex keys are unchanged.

Retry restores health and the chapter checkpoint while retaining the current expedition ledger. Weapon reset always cancels held draw, pending casts/shots, reload timers and audio, then hydrates ammunition and equipment from that ledger. Continuing an unfinished quarry visit retains enemy health and kill claims and spawns at its safe entry. A new game starts a fresh ledger and clears its rewards and skills together.

`chapter-world.ts` has only optional damage/vitals callbacks so its original story apparition uses the same real armor and health. `weapons.ts` changes are the ammunition boundary, persistent selection/torch reset and equipment HUD availability. First-person weapon effects and aiming remain in their existing modules. The room reuses chapter stone/floor/trim materials, existing chest parts and `ink-apparition.glb`. Mira and the player share the Ante Apocalypse Y Bot rig.

Read-only diagnostics are `window.__ROOM_DEMO__.snapshot()` and `window.__EXPEDITION__.snapshot()`. There is no progression, damage or teleport setter exposed for browser tests.
